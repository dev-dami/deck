# Code of conduct

Deck is a small project, built in the open by people who care about their tools. We want it to be a place where anyone can ask a question, report a problem, or send a patch without fear of being belittled.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to Go.
- Assume good intent. Disagree about code, not about people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback, and be open to receiving it.
- Keep discussions on topic, on the issue or pull request in question.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone else's personal details, account information or private messages.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual content or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeatedly pushing a point after it has been answered.
- Posting credentials, tokens, or other people's private data, including in issues, pull requests and recordings.
- Any attempt to get the project to weaken the rules in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#playing-by-the-rules).

## Enforcement

The maintainer, [@dev-dami](https://github.com/dev-dami), is responsible for enforcing these standards. To report a concern, contact the maintainer on GitHub at [@dev-dami](https://github.com/dev-dami). If the matter is private, use the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or GitHub's built-in [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature, rather than posting the details in a public issue. The maintainer may remove or edit comments, close issues or pull requests, and block users from the repository, and will explain the decision where that is safe to do so.

Reports are handled confidentially.
